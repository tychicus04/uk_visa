## 📝 Tóm Tắt Feedback & Phương Pháp Thu Thập (300 từ)

**BETA TESTING OVERVIEW**

We conducted a 14-day closed beta test with 32 active participants (20 Android, 12 iOS) representing our target users: UK citizenship test candidates aged 25-45, with mixed English proficiency levels including 47% Vietnamese speakers.

**FEEDBACK COLLECTION METHODS**

We employed multiple comprehensive channels:

1. **In-app Feedback Forms** (75% response rate): Integrated directly in the app with post-test surveys collecting ratings and comments in real-time via Firebase.

2. **Google Forms Survey** (66% response rate): Detailed 35-question survey sent after 7 days covering feature usage, satisfaction ratings, bugs, and suggestions.

3. **Email Reports** (25% response rate): Dedicated support email for detailed bug reports with screenshots and videos.

4. **Analytics & Crash Reporting** (100% coverage): Automated tracking via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics monitoring user behavior, session duration, feature adoption, and crash patterns.

5. **One-on-One Interviews**: Six 30-minute video calls providing deep qualitative insights into user experience.

**KEY RESULTS**

Overall satisfaction: **4.3/5 stars** with **87.5% recommendation rate**. All core features tested thoroughly: Practice Mode (100% usage), Mock Exams (94%), Multi-language switching (88%), and Chapter Learning (81%).

**FEEDBACK HIGHLIGHTS**

Strengths: Multi-language support rated highest (4.7/5) - our key differentiator. Testers praised content accuracy, clean UI/UX, and offline functionality.

Issues Fixed: Resolved 10 bugs including 2 critical crashes (language switch, offline sync). Improved UX based on feedback: enhanced bookmark visibility, added progress on home screen, implemented "Save & Resume" for tests.

**USAGE VALIDATION**

Testers used the app matching expected real-world patterns: daily practice sessions (18.5 min average), mock test completion (78%), and high Day-7 retention (84%). The testing confirmed strong product-market fit and readiness for public release.
